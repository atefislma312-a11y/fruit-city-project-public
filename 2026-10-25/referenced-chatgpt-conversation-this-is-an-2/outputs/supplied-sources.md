### Honest gap (do not fake)
No freely-licensed photograph exists of the actual mango moments: workers receiving the basket, the wax mango in its glass vitrine, or the factory celebrations with the relic. Those photos live in commercial archives (Getty, etc.). The honest build uses real era photos for energy + the museum piece for the event itself. The story of 4 Aug 1968 (BBC: https://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-35461265, Cooper Hewitt: https://www.cooperhewitt.org/2018/06/13/gift-basket/) goes on the typeset story card instead.

### The verified story (for the story card)
4 Aug 1968: Pakistan's foreign minister Mian Arshad Hussain gifts Mao a basket of ~40 mangoes. Mao regifts them to the worker-peasant propaganda team at Tsinghua University. The mangoes are worshipped as relics: preserved in wax or formaldehyde, one flown to Shanghai, one boiled so every worker could sip a spoonful. The cult lasts about a year.
